Summary: Source System Consolidation

Today, front-end cost data lives across 7 disconnected sources — the Acquisition Form, CDK, Ramp, 3 separate Excel-based manual calculations, and one component with no tracking at all. This fragmentation makes it difficult to produce an accurate, timely GPU and creates reconciliation risk at close.

Phase 1 consolidates these into 2 primary systems: Paddock for all landed costs and Repair 360 for reconditioning (direct costs, parts, and tech labor). Two items remain open — porter/detailer labor allocation (TBD) and overhead allocation (continues in Excel). Resolving these and building the reconciliation bridge back to cash in the accounting system are key follow-ups.
